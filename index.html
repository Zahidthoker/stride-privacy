Stride Privacy Policy

Developer: zahidthoker
Contact: zahidthoker.work@gmail.com
Updated: 3 November 2026

Activity and health information

Stride stores phone step counts, calibration settings, and recorded walking/running routes and summaries on your device. Motion permission supports step counting. Precise location supports a workout you start, including screen-off recording with an ongoing notification. Stride does not operate a server for uploading your activity history.

With your permission, Stride reads supported heart rate, oxygen saturation, resting heart rate, respiratory rate and sleep-session records through Health Connect when you connect or refresh. Sources and timestamps are displayed. Stride does not write these records, sell them, or pass them to advertising requests. Health Connect access is used only for the fitness features you choose.

Maps

Google Maps receives requests for the geographic area you view and processes data according to Google's policies. Street maps need network access. You can switch to the local route trace. Map display does not change recorded GPS distances.

Advertising and privacy choices

Stride uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google's SDK may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, security, measurement and other purposes described by Google. Stride does not include your step counts, workout coordinates or Health Connect records in ad requests. Applicable consent choices are requested through Google's User Messaging Platform; ad privacy choices are available in History where required. Tracking features are available regardless of advertising availability.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Exports, retention and deletion

Exports share data with the destination you choose. GPX files contain route coordinates and times; summary exports contain daily steps and workout summaries. You control copies shared outside Stride. Finished workouts can be deleted from History. Clearing Stride's app storage or uninstalling removes its local activity records and settings. Android cloud backup is disabled. Health Connect records remain controlled by their original source and Health Connect settings; uninstalling Stride does not remove those source records.

Your controls

You may revoke motion, location, notifications and Health Connect permissions in Android settings. Revoking access may prevent the related features from working. Contact zahidthoker.work@gmail.com with privacy questions. Stride does not require an account.

Fitness limitations and updates

Stride provides fitness information, not medical diagnosis or treatment. Step distance is estimated and GPS measurements vary with conditions. This policy may be updated when features or data practices change; the current policy will show its update date.
